PERSONAL ARTIST CHANNEL 1: ERG0SPHERE
Artist and rights holder: KoSch / Kolja W. Schumann
Album: erg0sphere - journey discontinued
Source: https://soundcloud.com/koljawschumann/sets/erg0sphere-journey
External provider: SoundCloud
Privacy policy: https://soundcloud.com/pages/privacy
Cookie policy: https://soundcloud.com/pages/cookies

The ERG0SPHERE station never automatically downloads, plays or connects to SoundCloud. The external browser opens only following an explicit tap on the SoundCloud album button. SoundCloud may then receive an IP address, browser/device information and cookie or account-related information. Its own privacy and cookie policies govern this processing. The separate optional HTML embed also remains inactive until the visitor explicitly authorizes it and does not enable autoplay.

The artist has confirmed their rights to the original album in the project discussion. No original album master files are currently packaged in the APK; an album link is neither a CC0 licence nor a licence to redistribute a third party's files.

The rights holder confirms their own reuse rights to this album in the project discussion. The Ska station is external-only until the original authorized audio masters are provided and validated for offline distribution. SoundCloud is opened through an explicit user action only. No background stream, automatic iframe, saved account credentials or hidden SoundCloud network request is made by SkiZones. The HTML example uses an explicit two-click consent model and auto_play=false. Opening SoundCloud may disclose IP/device information and permit SoundCloud cookies; the provider's policies apply.

OFFLINE RADIO AND THIRD-PARTY SOURCES
The separately bundled CC0 radio files are loaded locally. Each recording has an artist, source, licence and pinned integrity data in assets/music/cc0/library.json. Album material owned by the creator is never falsely presented as CC0. Some folk genres and the long-form atmospheric playlists are under continued genre/duration review. Before including new recordings, licence scope, original author, source recording and actual length must be verified.

These are technical notices for the software and are not a fully customized, legally reviewed privacy policy.
