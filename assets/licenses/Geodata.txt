terrain rather than a survey of current snow or individual buildings.

Geographic names and lift types were checked against the operators'

Data completeness varies. OSM line geometry is used for playable pistes;
area outlines alone are not converted into invented piste centre lines.
Imported piste colours use OSM difficulty tags when available; new pistes
use the slope model. Generated pedestrian access, plazas, buildings and
forest decoration are game additions. Staffing numbers,
wages, outages and scenario goals are fictional simulation parameters.
