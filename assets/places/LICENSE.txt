Each .bin is a deterministic gzip-compressed UTF-8 JSON dataset. The adjacent
.json records its source timestamp, original Overpass-response SHA-256,
projection origin and distribution SHA-256. Rebuild with tools/import_places.py.
Building footprints, streets and named POIs are open geographic data. Rendered
facades, extra resort shuttles and simulation timetables are game interpretations.
