# SkiZones 0.40.0 – Alpine Renaissance (development branch)

**Status: in development; NOT a released APK.** Version 0.39.1 remains the independent stabilization release. Do not merge this branch into `main` or change `project.godot` / Android versionCode until 0.39.1 is published and verified. The existing historical `docs/releases/0.4.0.md` refers to a much earlier version 0.4.0 (versionCode 5); this is the new **0.40.0** milestone, not that old release.

## Design direction

A believable, lively Alpine resort management game rather than a toy-like dashboard: detailed mountains and villages, beautiful but readable snow surfaces, atmospheric daylight, elegant glass-and-metal panels, tactile mobile controls, understandable business data and animated staff/guest feedback. Keep offline-first play, Android performance and backwards-compatible saves as non-negotiable constraints.

## Milestones / acceptance criteria

### A. Alpine world and rendering
- **Started in this branch:** readable compacted-snow footpaths against snow terrain, with soft snowbank edges, preserved walkability and distinct gravel/timber/paved surfaces. Independent contrast regression `tests/snow_path_visibility_040.gd`.
- Seamless, rounded joins between footpaths, ski pistes, lift plazas and hotel forecourts; no clipped or vanishing path ends. Verify with rendered before/after captures at phone and tablet sizes.
- More detailed forest diversity, alpine rocks, lighting, weather, seasonally randomized Christmas/New-Year billboards; no distracting particle overload.
- Consistent visual styles, including modern alpine, realistic, monochrome and illustrated variants; each must preserve UI and route contrast.

### B. Major UX/UI redesign
- Rework top HUD and construction palettes for 16:9 and tall phone screens, with large touch targets, safe-area padding, consistent iconography and collapsible contextual actions.
- Replace long building descriptions with visual KPIs (occupancy, satisfaction, income, maintenance, staffing, queues). Put frequent actions at the top; keep detailed stats expandable.
- Improve load/save/repair with clear slot identity, origin-version badge, validation status, explicit backup/restore previews and readable delete controls. Never silently overwrite an unreadable original.
- Radio as a polished hardware-like control panel with wrapping stations, fully visible power control, coherent volume controls, station genre branding and accurate track status.
- Hotel mode and building/team/appearance modules should be accessible from the building inspector without deep scrolling.

### C. Gameplay depth and simulation
- Hotel management: floors, room classes, extensions (spa, pool, fitness, restaurants), guests and staff; public WC management remains independent.
- Single purchasable Portable Toilet with five visually distinct upgrade tiers, upgrade tree and retroactive load of all historic toilet kinds.
- Guest groups/families with distinct skill and needs, realistic lift queues and rerouting, nonblocking walking paths, accessible restaurants and facilities.
- More visible lift maintenance, staffing, breakdown recovery and mountain transport connections.
- Free mode: unlimited funds, unlocked building/research options, independent save slots; career mode: progression and meaningful economic balancing.
- Distinct resort missions and scenario settings, without breaking main-mode gameplay.

### D. Technical gates and delivery
- Keep a permanent corpus of real saved-game fixtures from every available historical release; reject modern checksum corruption, allow valid pre-checksum formats and preserve damaged originals.
- Maintain in-place Android APK upgrade signing identity, package name, monotonic versionCode and old saves on upgrade.
- Add visual regression captures for snow paths, path-to-piste joins, radio and building inspector on at least two phone aspect ratios.
- Performance budget: no unbounded mesh/cell generation, batch distant scenery, retain mobile touch responsiveness; record baseline and comparison.
- Run all existing Godot suites, historical saves, career recovery, Android lifecycle tests and export/signature checks before a public v0.40.0 release.

## E. Procedural Map Creator — flagship 0.40 addition

**Implemented in the development branch (pending full Android/regression QA):**
- Deterministic seed-based terrain blueprint, four map sizes, seven terrain types (ranges, independent peaks, high plateaus, alms, hills, glacial basins, horseshoe valleys).
- Peak count and height, forest density, explicit tree population, lakes, villages, mountain bearing, valley/plateau width, rolling hills, snow line, climate, slope exposure, terrain difficulty, erosion and avalanche pressure. Preview is generated from actual peak geometry.
- Free-build creation without campaign requirements; mission/career creation locked until all eight fantasy areas are unlocked and all four real scenario gold medals are earned.
- Eight individually named playable custom landscapes, a checksum-verified atomic map registry, separate career and free-play save namespaces and predictable relaunch identifiers. Historical pre-custom saves remain unaltered.
- Real ForestLOD uses the configured tree-population cap for custom worlds while preserving existing resort forests.

**Remaining gameplay/quality work:**
- Test end-to-end new/custom/save/relaunch on physical Android, free/career contexts, unlock trophy update, full-size mobile performance.
- Render configurable lakes, villages, season/snowline, erosion/weather and avalanche hazards as actual simulation elements (not only blueprint metadata). Validate geography and mountain access.
- Add map-library archive, export/import and preview of save occupancy before allowing replacement/removal; no silent deletion.
- Improve playable starter infrastructure placement across every extreme terrain combination (avoid cliffs and blocked lift/piste starts).
- Max tree count is a bounded population target; fewer trees may appear where terrain, cliffs, access roads, piste corridors and snowline exclude planting. Display the *actual placed count*, never pretend a requested maximum is always achieved.

## F. Venue-specific specialist workforce — 0.40

**Implemented in branch, with targeted Godot regression:**
- Restaurant uses chef and waiter; hotel reception, housekeeping and security; bar uses bartender; ski school instructor; pool lifeguard; spa therapist; equipment hire fitter; retail vendor; workshop artisan and other industry-specific occupations.
- Hiring **and reassignment** to a wrong building type are rejected. Old saved catering/hotel crews remain eligible as baseline employees rather than breaking historical resorts.
- Actual current-shift qualified personnel improve per-building visitor capacity and shorten per-guest service processing time; expertise, experience and motivation give capped diminishing productivity bonuses.
- Rescue stations have day/evening/night staffing rosters.

**Remaining:** display qualified productivity %, active team and changed queue duration in building inspectors; validate performance and fairness of role-based staffing across older saves, non-food industries and long-running simulations; ensure hiring UX filters workplace choices by role.

## G. Alpine rescue / ski patrol — 0.40

**Implemented in branch, pending rendered/device QA:**
- Dispatch the nearest **available pair of on-duty rescue workers**, pathfinding over actual playable mountain routes (not straight-line teleports). Each crew is busy until evacuation/guidance ends. Eligible distant scenes can use an idle compact snowcat.
- Ski patrol travels outbound with a folded stretcher; a casualty lies visibly on snow and then on the returning stretcher. A small attention marker remains at the incident. Rescued casualties receive **60–120 in-game minutes** of station treatment before returning to the guest system.
- Fear of a dangerous descent is a **separate guidance incident**: standing frightened skiers receive escort rather than being carried as injured patients; person-context states show fear, injury, rescue transport and treatment progress.
- Night/shift coverage and deterministic saved rescue stages.

**Remaining before marking complete:** confirm 3D model fidelity, ski physics and smooth transitions on actual routes (including ascending tracked mini snowcats and snowcat availability reservations); link skischool instructors who pass near a frightened guest into the guidance assignment; allow interrupted rescue handovers at staffing changes without teleportation. Add broad end-to-end tests and visual/Android screenshots of each stage.
