# SkiZones 0.40.0 – Alpine Renaissance (development branch)

**Status: in development; NOT a released APK.** Version 0.39.1 remains the independent stabilization release. Do not merge this branch into `main` or change `project.godot` / Android versionCode until 0.39.1 is published and verified. The existing historical `docs/releases/0.4.0.md` refers to a much earlier version 0.4.0 (versionCode 5); this is the new **0.40.0** milestone, not that old release.

## Design direction

A believable, lively Alpine resort management game rather than a toy-like dashboard: detailed mountains and villages, beautiful but readable snow surfaces, atmospheric daylight, elegant glass-and-metal panels, tactile mobile controls, understandable business data and animated staff/guest feedback. Keep offline-first play, Android performance and backwards-compatible saves as non-negotiable constraints.

## Milestones / acceptance criteria

### A. Alpine world and rendering
- **Started in this branch:** readable compacted-snow footpaths against snow terrain, with soft snowbank edges, preserved walkability and distinct gravel/timber/paved surfaces. Independent contrast regression `tests/snow_path_visibility_040.gd`.
- Seamless, rounded joins between footpaths, ski pistes, lift plazas and hotel forecourts; no clipped or vanishing path ends. Verify with rendered before/after captures at phone and tablet sizes.
- More detailed forest diversity, alpine rocks, lighting, weather, seasonally randomized Christmas/New-Year billboards; no distracting particle overload.
- Consistent visual styles, including modern alpine, realistic, monochrome and illustrated variants; each must preserve UI and route contrast.

### B. Major UX/UI redesign
- Rework top HUD and construction palettes for 16:9 and tall phone screens, with large touch targets, safe-area padding, consistent iconography and collapsible contextual actions.
- Replace long building descriptions with visual KPIs (occupancy, satisfaction, income, maintenance, staffing, queues). Put frequent actions at the top; keep detailed stats expandable.
- Improve load/save/repair with clear slot identity, origin-version badge, validation status, explicit backup/restore previews and readable delete controls. Never silently overwrite an unreadable original.
- Radio as a polished hardware-like control panel with wrapping stations, fully visible power control, coherent volume controls, station genre branding and accurate track status.
- Hotel mode and building/team/appearance modules should be accessible from the building inspector without deep scrolling.

### C. Gameplay depth and simulation
- Hotel management: floors, room classes, extensions (spa, pool, fitness, restaurants), guests and staff; public WC management remains independent.
- Single purchasable Portable Toilet with five visually distinct upgrade tiers, upgrade tree and retroactive load of all historic toilet kinds.
- Guest groups/families with distinct skill and needs, realistic lift queues and rerouting, nonblocking walking paths, accessible restaurants and facilities.
- More visible lift maintenance, staffing, breakdown recovery and mountain transport connections.
- Free mode: unlimited funds, unlocked building/research options, independent save slots; career mode: progression and meaningful economic balancing.
- Distinct resort missions and scenario settings, without breaking main-mode gameplay.

### D. Technical gates and delivery
- Keep a permanent corpus of real saved-game fixtures from every available historical release; reject modern checksum corruption, allow valid pre-checksum formats and preserve damaged originals.
- Maintain in-place Android APK upgrade signing identity, package name, monotonic versionCode and old saves on upgrade.
- Add visual regression captures for snow paths, path-to-piste joins, radio and building inspector on at least two phone aspect ratios.
- Performance budget: no unbounded mesh/cell generation, batch distant scenery, retain mobile touch responsiveness; record baseline and comparison.
- Run all existing Godot suites, historical saves, career recovery, Android lifecycle tests and export/signature checks before a public v0.40.0 release.

## New: map creator, professional venue crews and ski patrol (0.40 implementation)

### Map Creator – in progress, not yet a playable custom save
- Four map sizes, seven landform families (ridges, individual mountains, plateaus, alpine pastures, hills, glacial basins, horseshoe valleys), up to 24 mountains and 2,800 m specified peaks.
- Controllable peak count/height, mountain connectivity and orientation, valley width, plateaus, tree count/forest area/tree line, lakes, villages, rivers, trailheads and rescue access, erosion, exposure, snow line, climate, difficulty and avalanche risk.
- Reproducible seeded generation. New generated geographical records place lakes, village hubs, river segments, trailhead access and bounded forest clusters on the map; the topographic preview shows the actual generated positions.
- Free mode opens the editor without prerequisites; the mission unlock currently requires every main resort unlocked plus all four real-world scenario gold medals. The editor saves a checksum-protected draft.
- **Still outstanding before release:** integration into playable 3D terrain, distinct editable custom map save slots, collision and lift/path placement validation on custom terrain, actual river/water mesh and clustered tree instancing, recovery if blueprint data is damaged. Never call a preview a playable custom map.

### Real building occupations – first implementation
- Sector-aware optional jobs: chefs/waiters (restaurants), reception/housekeeping (hotels), ski instructors, shop specialists, pool lifeguards, spa therapists and rescue paramedics.
- New assignments reject mismatched roles; staff on duty earn their actual wages and improve venue throughput, real guest capacity and service quality. Tired or less qualified workers deliver smaller gains; hard caps prevent artificial infinite capacity.
- The previously published generic roles and salary data remain loadable for old resorts. Existing mandatory staffing minimums are not silently increased.

### Mountain rescue – staged prototype
- Actual two-person staffed patrols and one dispatch team per incident. Select the fastest **available** reachable station/team, not merely any first aid building. Dispatch paths use the existing ski/pedestrian/lift routing graph and are stored with the incident.
- Snowcat mode for upgraded stations when its routed approach avoids lifts and the weather/time/distance justifies it. A two-skier model with empty outbound stretcher, loaded return stretcher, smaller tracked-rescue vehicle, fallen patient and visible amber/red attention indicators is present.
- Separate cases: `navigation` is fear requiring escort (no fictional injury or medical treatment); collision/fatigue/evacuation require transport, 60–120 simulated minutes of medical care, and then return from the first-aid station. Night rescue uses actual staffed night shifts.
- Guest context panels distinguish waiting, guided descent, stretcher return and treatment progress.
- **Still outstanding before release:** full live scene validation, patient pickup/drop-off animation, route-following correctness with all lift/piste combinations, ski-school accompaniment during fear incidents, actual separate rescue snowcat purchases and dispatch, proper guest/family animation during transport, historical save fixtures for new incident statuses, Android FPS budgets.

### Verification and release guardrails
- Focused workflow `.github/workflows/map-staff-rescue-040.yml` runs localization, headless import/parse and the new regression tests.
- None of these 0.40 changes go into the stabilization release 0.39.1. A 0.40 APK must not be published before all technical and game-scene tests pass.
